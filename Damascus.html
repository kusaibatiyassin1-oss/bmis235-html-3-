Damascus

Damascus is my favorite city in Syria. It's called the city of Jasmine due to the high frequency of jasmines.

What I love about it is that it has everything. It has an ancient city feeling with a hint of modernization.

Some of Damascus's most known attractions would be the Ummayaad Mosque, Citadel and it's famous sword in the heart of Damascus.

This city gives me a feeling I can't explain. This is why this is possibly my favorite city in the world.
